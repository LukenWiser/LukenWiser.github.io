About the studio

Driftwire Games is a one-person studio founded by Luken Wiser, who designs and directs every game we make.

The name comes from the logo: a single live wire that drifts, bends into a D and ends in a spark. That is how we like games to feel: quick to pick up, full of energy, and always moving.

Everything we make runs in a web browser, so anyone can play from a link, on a school laptop, a phone or a gaming PC.
